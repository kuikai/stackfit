Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Stack Fit is a workout timer. You can use it without an account. This page describes what is stored, and how to delete an account.

On the phone

Workouts, timers, history, and appearance settings are saved on the device. The app does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.

Account

An account is optional. If you create one, the server stores:

Your email address. A scrambled copy of your password, which cannot be read back. Your display name, if you set one. The workouts, history, custom exercises, and appearance settings you sync. Share links you create.

That information is kept so you can open the same workouts on another device, and so someone with a share link can open the workout or progress you chose to share. A share page does not show your email.

The app does not sell this information.

Delete an account

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and enter your password.

On this website, sign in, choose Delete account, and enter your password.

Deletion removes the account, the workouts and history stored for it, and its share links. Copies already saved on a phone stay on that phone until you remove them there.

Contact

The contact for this app is the email address on the Google Play store listing.
