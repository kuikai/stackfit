# Skin ideas

A parking lot for skins we might make later. Nothing here is promised.
When one ships, move it to the **Done** list at the bottom.

## How a skin is built

- **Everyday skin** (free): colours + font + corners only.
  Add it in `lib/core/skins.dart`, `skin_palettes.dart`, and `skin_looks.dart`,
  then add it to the list in `test/skin_look_test.dart`.
- **Pro skin**: everything above, plus artwork under `StackFitWeb/themes/<name>/`
  (banner, background, girl-standing, mascots, run frames). See
  `StackFitWeb/themes/README.md`.

Keep brand names out of the app. A skin can look *like* a drink, a game, or a
team, but the name and logo must be our own, or the App Store may reject it.
